# v0.1.0

2026-10-23 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

- Keep your Mac awake from the menu bar: Start, or one click on 15 min, 1 hour, 2 hours, Until… or Custom…. The candle in the menu bar burns down as time runs out.
- Stay awake while an app or command is running, such as Xcode, claude or ffmpeg. Add them under **Stay awake while** in the popover.
- See what else keeps your Mac awake, with the reason, for example "caffeinate · Started by claude".
- Stops below 20% battery, and optionally in Low Power Mode. Change it in **Settings → Battery**.
- **Keep display on** sits in the popover. A global shortcut, launch at login and what Start begins are in **Settings → General**.
- Wick checks for updates once a day and installs only when you choose, from **Settings → Updates**.
