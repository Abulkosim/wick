# v0.3.0

2026-10-25 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.3.0)

- Right-click the candle to keep awake for 15 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours or until stopped, or to stop, without opening the popover.
- **Add 15 min** on the status line extends a timed session instead of restarting it.
- In the popover, press 1, 2 or 3 for the presets, 0 for until stopped, S to start or stop, and + to add 15 minutes.
- A session keeps running through the restart after an in-app update.
- The shortcut and a click on the icon now stop a trigger that's keeping your Mac awake, instead of starting a session on top of it.
- An external display that's asleep or mirrored still counts for the external display condition.
- Wick does less work in the background: it only looks for other apps and the lid while the popover is open.
