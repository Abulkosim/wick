# v0.5.0

2026-10-27 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.5.0)

- Stay awake while the network or CPU is busy: add **Network busy** or **CPU busy** under Stay awake while → Add → Condition, and pick how busy counts (100 KB/s, 1 MB/s or 10 MB/s; 10, 25 or 50%). Wick lets go once it's been quiet for 2 minutes.
- The Awake today card takes one row, and the popover grows taller before it scrolls, so more triggers fit.
- A Raycast extension starts, stops and checks Wick, including typed durations like 45 or 16:30. Its source is in the repository's `raycast/` folder; it isn't in the Raycast Store yet.
