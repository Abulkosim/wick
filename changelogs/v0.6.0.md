# v0.6.0

2026-10-31 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.6.0)

- Wick can tell you when a trigger stops keeping your Mac awake, for example "Xcode quit. Your Mac can sleep again." or "Unplugged. Your Mac can sleep again." Turn it on in Settings → Notifications → When a trigger ends.
- It stays quiet when you press Stop, while a session is running, or when another trigger still keeps the Mac awake.
