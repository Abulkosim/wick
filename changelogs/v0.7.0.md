# v0.7.0

2026-11-05 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.7.0)

- Wick runs on Linux: an AppImage for any distribution, plus `.deb` and `.rpm` packages, for x86_64 with glibc 2.35 or newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and later). See [Install → Linux](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/blob/main/docs/install.md#linux).
- On Linux the candle sits in the panel's tray: left-click opens Wick, right-click shows the menu. Without a tray (GNOME without the AppIndicator extension), Wick opens as a normal window.
- Wick keeps Linux awake through your desktop's own inhibitor and systemd-logind, so `systemd-inhibit --list` shows it, and quitting or a crash lets the computer sleep again. "Who else" lists other apps holding it awake.
- The AppImage updates itself; the `.deb` and `.rpm` update through a password prompt. `wick://` links and the `wick` command work on Linux too.
- Windows and Linux show shortcuts as Ctrl+Alt+W instead of Mac symbols, and messages say PC or computer instead of Mac.
