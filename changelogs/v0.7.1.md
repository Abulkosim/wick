# v0.7.1

2026-11-05 · [Download](https://github.com/Abulkosim/wick/releases/tag/v0.7.1)

- On Xfce and other desktops whose portal can't block suspend, **Keep display on** now really keeps the screen on.
- Without a tray, closing Wick's window quits Wick and ends the session, instead of hiding it with the session still running. Esc no longer hides it.
- A USB-C charger with programmable power (USB PD PPS) counts as plugged in, so the battery guard and the power condition don't act while charging.
- Laptops with two batteries show the right charge level when the batteries report it differently.
- Wick no longer stalls when the desktop's D-Bus services are slow to answer.
